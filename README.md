# The Glovebox & Backpack Game Book

I made this for my nephew! Fun games to play around the campground or on road trips.

Live at [games.myjimmycloud.com](https://games.myjimmycloud.com). One page, `index.html`, no build; the games run in the browser and only the fonts come from Google Fonts: print your favorites before you leave, or tap and play on the phone. Every sheet fills one US Letter page in portrait.

## What it prints

**Make & print** (a fresh one every time):

- **Sudoku**: single-solution puzzles in four difficulties, one per page.
- **Spotting Bingo**: cards in nine themes, Road Trip to Beach Day, as many as the group needs.
- **DIY Bingo**: bingo cards from your own list.
- **Word Search**: themed letter grids with the word list.
- **Word Hunt**: Boggle-style 4×4 and 5×5 letter grids.
- **Cryptograms**: coded proverbs, answers printed upside-down.
- **Mazes**: small, medium and large, always solvable.

**Print & go:** a Battleship sheet per captain, the License Plates checklist of all 50 states, and a Yahtzee scorecard.

**Keep score:** a cribbage board, one 121-hole track for 2, 3 or 4 players.

**Rule books:** card rules (poker hands and 14 games), ten dice games, paper-and-pencil games, car and travel games that need nothing, and campfire games.

**Booklet Builder:** pick how many of each, or a preset (Day trip, Long haul, The works), and print the whole stack at once to clip together.
